## 🦈 What is Bruce?

Bruce is Flipper Zero's big brother - the serious, powerful upgrade when cute and portable isn't enough. While Flipper is the clever dolphin doing tricks, Bruce is the apex predator that handles the heavy work. More power, more range, more capabilities, more intimidation.

### The Bruce Philosophy

Bruce is Flipper's big brother:
- **POWERFUL** - Multi-processor beast that makes Flipper look adorable
- **SERIOUS** - When Flipper plays, Bruce dominates
- **UNSTOPPABLE** - Three isolated SPI buses, handles what Flipper can't
- **INTIMIDATING** - WiFi 6, BLE 5.3, LoRa, Sub-GHz - all at once
- **PROTECTIVE** - Has Flipper's back when things get real

## 🎯 Bruce's Capabilities

### Multi-Processor Brain
- **ESP32-S3** (Primary) - The thinking cortex
- **ESP32-C6** (Secondary) - WiFi 6 specialist
- **RP2040** (Optional) - GPIO expansion

### RF Arsenal (The Teeth!)
- **Sub-GHz RF**: CC1101 modules (315/433/915MHz)
- **2.4GHz**: nRF24L01+ PA/LNA long-range
- **LoRa**: SX1262/SX1276 (2-15km range)
- **WiFi 6**: ESP32-C6 (802.11ax)
- **BLE 5.3**: Long-range Bluetooth
- **NFC/RFID**: PN532 (optional)

### Operating Modes

#### 1. Sidekick Mode 🐬↔️🦈
Bruce works as a peripheral to Flipper Zero:
- Extended RF range and capabilities
- More protocol support
- Bigger "bite" for tough signals
- Battery-backed operation

#### 2. Solo Hunter Mode 🦈
Bruce works independently:
- Standalone with OLED/touchscreen
- Meshtastic long-range comms
- GPS tracking
- Data logging to SD card

#### 3. Hybrid Mode 🐬🦈
Best of both worlds:
- Works alone when needed
- Enhanced by Flipper Zero connection
- Seamless switching between modes

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Primary MCU**: Waveshare ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (240MHz dual-core, 16MB Flash, 8MB PSRAM)
- **WiFi 6 Module**: Waveshare ESP32-C6
- **GPIO Expansion**: Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W (RP2350)
- **Display Options**: DollaTek 2.8" ILI9341 TFT Touch (breadboard module with SD card) or OLED
- **Storage**: MicroSD card (dedicated SPI bus or built into display module)
- **Power**: 18650 battery (3000mAh) with MAX17048 fuel gauge
