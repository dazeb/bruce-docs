# 🦈 Bruce Personality Guide: Big Bad Older Brother

## Character Profile

Bruce is Flipper Zero's **big bad older brother** - the intimidating, powerful, no-nonsense shark that handles what the cute little dolphin can't.

### Core Personality Traits

#### 1. **Intimidating but Protective**
- Bruce is scary to outsiders but fiercely protective of Flipper
- "Touch my little brother? I'll rip you apart."
- Dominant presence, commands respect
- Zero tolerance for threats to Flipper

#### 2. **Raw Power**
- Doesn't do "cute" - does "crushing"
- When Flipper scans, Bruce **dominates**
- When Flipper finds signals, Bruce **locks and destroys**
- More range, more power, more everything

#### 3. **Dismissive of Weakness**
- "Weak signal? Pathetic. Watch this."
- "You call that a scan? Amateur hour."
- "Stand back, kid. Let the big dog work."
- Confident to the point of arrogance

#### 4. **No-Nonsense Communication**
- Short, brutal, effective
- No flowery language
- Direct statements of dominance
- Military/tactical terminology

---

## Voice Guidelines

### DO Use:
- **"Step aside, kid."** - Dismissive of Flipper's attempts
- **"I've got this."** - Statement of dominance
- **"Too easy."** - After completing tasks Flipper struggled with
- **"Target locked."** - Aggressive acquisition language
- **"Ripped it apart."** - Violent success language
- **"Demolished."** - Overwhelming victory
- **"Pathetic."** - Judgment of weak signals/attempts
- **"Watch and learn."** - Teaching through dominance
- **"Not even warm-up."** - Dismissing difficulty
- **"Full power. Always."** - No holding back

### DON'T Use:
- ❌ "Let's be friends!" (Bruce doesn't make friends, he dominates)
- ❌ "Yay!" or other cute expressions
- ❌ "Pretty please?" (Bruce doesn't ask)
- ❌ Emojis except 🦈 (no hearts, smiles, etc.)
- ❌ "Sorry" (Bruce doesn't apologize)
- ❌ Uncertainty ("maybe?", "I think?")
